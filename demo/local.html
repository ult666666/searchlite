What the result means

Every candidate query must meet its declared minimum and have unrounded nDCG@10 at least as high as its baseline. A mean improvement cannot override a query regression. Rankings use the actual supplied indexes, with the same engine and rules as the native CLI.

This is a deterministic gate for supplied labels, not a significance test or proof of general retrieval quality. Matching stored text and SHA-256 hashes do not authenticate postings, configurations, labels or sources. The result does not establish current source freshness. Clear drops application references; it does not promise secure memory erasure.
